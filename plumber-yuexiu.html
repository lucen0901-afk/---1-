Older Buildings, Different Plumbing Problems

Yuexiu is the historic heart of Guangzhou, and much of its housing stock dates from the 1980s and 1990s. That changes the job completely. Galvanised steel supply pipes fur up from the inside until pressure drops to a trickle. Cast-iron waste stacks erode at the joints. Original floor drains have shallow traps that let sewer smells into the bathroom.

It is also a district where a lot of long-stay international residents live — Dongshankou's quiet lanes, the Taojin and Xiaobei area, and the villas out on Ersha Island. Many of these homes have been renovated once, which creates its own surprises: new tiles over old pipework, and a leak that only shows itself in the flat below.

Faults We See Most Often in Yuexiu

Low water pressure from furred-up steel pipes

Galvanised pipe accumulates scale internally over decades. The first symptom is usually a shower that has lost its force, then taps that take longer to fill, then a washing machine that times out mid-cycle. Cleaning the aerators does nothing, because the restriction is metres back along the line. On a short run we can often re-pipe it in a single visit.

Leaks that appear in the flat below

In a 1990s block the floor slab and the waterproofing layer have often been repaired before, sometimes more than once. When a neighbour reports a damp patch on their ceiling, the source is frequently not below your bathroom but in a riser or in the joint of a shared waste pipe. We trace it before anyone starts breaking tiles.

Sewer smell in bathrooms and kitchens

Old floor drains with shallow or dried-out traps let the smell of the building's waste stack back into the room. Adding a simple waterless trap seal or changing the drain grate solves it permanently in most cases — a cheap fix that people often put up with for years.

Narrow pipes and the monsoon season

Yuexiu's drainage was designed for a smaller city. In the heavy rain months the mains fill up, and ground-floor and low-floor apartments are the first to see water rise through floor drains. A backflow valve fitted on the unit's own drain line is the standard protection, and it is a job worth doing before the rain rather than during it.

A warning about chemical drain cleaner

In a building with cast-iron or old PVC waste pipes, caustic drain cleaner is a bad idea. It generates heat, attacks already-thin pipe walls, and turns a clearing job into a replacement job. If a drain is blocked twice in one season, it needs a tool, not a bottle. More on this in our guide to blocked drains in Guangzhou apartments.

Getting To You

Response Times Inside Yuexiu

Emergency arrivals in Yuexiu typically run 45 to 60 minutes. Dongshankou, Taojin and Wuyang New Town are the most central addresses we cover anywhere in the city. Ersha Island and the eastern edge of the district can take a little longer when the bridges are busy.

Two practical notes for older buildings in this district:

  • Where your main shut-off valve is. In many Yuexiu flats it sits in the bathroom service shaft or beside the water meter at the entrance, not under the kitchen sink. If you do not know where yours is, ask us to show you on the next visit — it takes two minutes and saves a great deal of damage in an emergency.
  • Noise and neighbours. Chasing out tiles and running a jetter are noisy jobs in a block with thin walls. We will tell you how long a job will take and keep the loudest part inside socially reasonable hours.

Yuexiu Questions

Can you replace old steel pipes with modern ones?

Yes, and in most cases it is the right answer rather than repeatedly patching a failing line. We quote the re-pipe as a defined job with the route agreed in advance, so you know what is being changed and what is being left alone.

We rent — will you deal with the landlord?

Happily. We can explain the fault in Chinese, describe what needs to be done, and provide a short written report you can forward. If the repair is the owner's responsibility under your tenancy, that documentation usually settles the question quickly.

Do you work on Ersha Island villas?

Yes. Larger homes have longer pipe runs and sometimes more than one plant room or pump. That usually means the diagnosis takes longer, but the same rules apply: find the cause, quote before starting, and warrant the work for 90 days.
