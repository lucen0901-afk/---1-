A District of Two Halves

Haizhu runs from the riverfront towers of Binjiang East down to the exhibition halls of Pazhou, and it packs very different housing into that strip. Along the river you have modern high-rises with booster pumps, pressure-reducing valves and sealed risers. Inland, around Kecun, Lujiang and the back streets off Jiangnan West, you have low-rise blocks and lane houses where the pipework is older, the runs are longer, and access is tighter.

Then there is Pazhou. Serviced apartments, hotel residences and short-let flats cluster around the Canton Fair complex, and they run on a schedule that does not allow for a slow drip becoming a bigger problem. When a guest is arriving tomorrow, a blocked shower drain is not next week's job.

Faults We See Most Often in Haizhu

Scale build-up in riverside towers

Guangzhou's tap water is hard, and it leaves scale in exactly the places you cannot see: the inside of shower mixers, the inlet of a washing machine, the element of an instant water heater. The symptom is usually a hot tap that runs weaker than the cold one, or a shower that fluctuates between scalding and cold. Descaling the affected fitting is a short job; letting it run for another year usually ends with a replacement instead.

Blocked shower and balcony drains in rented flats

Hair and soap in a shower trap, dust and laundry lint in a balcony drain. These are mundane problems that become urgent when a landlord inspection or a guest arrival is two days away. We keep evening and weekend slots open for precisely this reason.

Fast-turnaround work in Pazhou serviced apartments

During exhibition periods the demand for serviced accommodation in Pazhou is heavy, and standards are high. A running toilet, a dripping shower head or a slow basin needs to be resolved between guests, not during their stay. Tell us the check-out and check-in times when you book and we will work inside that window.

Main line backflow in low-rise blocks

In the older inland streets, shallow ground-floor units share a waste line with everyone above them. Grease and debris settle in the horizontal run, and the first sign is a gurgling floor drain followed by water rising through it. This is a main-line problem, not a local one, and it needs jetting — pouring cleaner down the drain only pushes the blockage further along.

Water coming through the ceiling?

Riverside high-rises have a lot of pipework in the ceiling void and in the shaft beside the bathroom. If you are seeing damp patches, our emergency guide covers what to do in the first ten minutes — including where the shut-off valve usually hides.

Haizhu Questions

Do you handle short-notice work for rental hosts?

Yes. A large share of our Haizhu work is for serviced apartments and short-let hosts who need a fix between bookings. Send the fault and the window you have available, and we will confirm whether we can meet it.

Is hard water damaging my fittings?

It is a factor, certainly. Scale shortens the life of mixers, valves and heater elements. If you are replacing a fitting anyway, it is worth asking for one that tolerates hard water, and worth descaling the shower and heater on a routine rather than waiting for a failure.

My floor drain smells. Is that a blockage?

Not necessarily. In older blocks it is often a dried-out or shallow trap allowing stack air back into the room. That is a small fix, not an emergency — but it is worth doing, because a reminder of the sewer every morning gets old quickly.
