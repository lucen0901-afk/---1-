Our Story

Built for expats, by people who understand

Guangzhou Home Help started in 2017 with a simple observation: foreign residents in Guangzhou were struggling with basic home repairs. Not because the work was complicated — but because finding someone reliable who spoke English felt impossible.

Our founder, a veteran plumber with over 15 years of experience, had spent years helping expat friends navigate plumbing emergencies late at night, translating repair terms, and negotiating fair prices on their behalf. It was clear there had to be a better way.

Today, we've grown into a full-service home maintenance company with a team of certified technicians — all trained to work with international clients. We still hold onto the values we started with: clear communication, fair pricing, and treating every home like it's our own.

Our Mission

What drives us every day

We believe everyone deserves to feel safe and comfortable in their home — no matter what language they speak or where they come from.

Break Down Language Barriers

Every interaction — from booking to billing — happens in clear, fluent English. No translation apps, no misunderstandings, no stress.

Deliver Trusted Quality

Our technicians are certified, background-checked, and held to the highest standards. We stand behind every job with a 90-day workmanship warranty.

Practice Transparent Pricing

You get the full price before any work starts. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons, no "while we're here" upsells. Just honest, fair pricing.
